## About the Project

The A.M Turai Travel & Tours Transportation Management System is a web-based system developed to help manage transportation activities in an organized and efficient way.

The system manages important transportation information such as:

- Vehicles
- Drivers
- Passengers
- Routes
- Trips
- Bookings
- Payments
- Reports
- Customer accounts

The system replaces manual record keeping with a centralized computerized system.

---

## Problem Statement

Transportation companies handle large amounts of information involving vehicles, drivers, passengers, routes, trips, bookings, and payments.

When these records are managed manually using notebooks, receipts, spreadsheets, or separate files, it can become difficult to keep information accurate and up to date.

This may result in:

- Duplicate bookings
- Incorrect passenger records
- Vehicle scheduling conflicts
- Driver scheduling conflicts
- Difficulty tracking payments
- Difficulty checking available seats
- Slow retrieval of previous trip information
- Delays in preparing reports
- Difficulty monitoring transportation activities

---

## Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a centralized Transportation Management System that allows administrators and customers to manage transportation activities through a web-based platform.

The system provides:

- Secure administrator login
- Customer registration and login
- Vehicle management
- Driver management
- Passenger management
- Route management
- Trip scheduling
- Booking management
- Payment records
- Reports
- Customer profile management
